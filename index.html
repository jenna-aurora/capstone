Meet the Justices

A student needs to find trends in Supreme Court justice appointments because they want to understand how the Court has changed over time. My page will help them explore justices by state president, and years of service.

Overview

This application allows users to explore information about Supreme Court justices, including the president who appointed them, their home state, and their years of service. Users can identify trends and compare appointments throughout Supreme Court history.
