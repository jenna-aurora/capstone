          A student needs to find trends in Supreme Court justice appointments because they want to understand how the Court has changed over time. My page will help them explore justices by state, president, and Tenure Length.         

      
      
        

Overview

        

The Judicial Trends Interactice Dashboard is a web application designed to analyze and explore historic patterns in Supreme Court Justice appointments. The platform is built to help users-specifically students-understand how the composition and characteristics of the Court have evolved over time.
